01 / Start

Start with what you have.

Start from old. 📱✨

You don’t need the latest phone, the best camera, or perfect resources to start.

I started my journey with a Samsung Galaxy J7. It wasn’t the best device, but it was enough to take the first step.

From an old phone to better equipment, from small beginnings to bigger dreams — the journey continues. ❤️

Whatever you have, start with it. Keep learning. Keep creating. Keep moving forward.

One day, you will look back and realize how far you’ve come. 🚀

02 / Editing

Video editing starts on the laptop.

This is where the story takes shape. I organize clips, refine the rhythm, add colour, and turn raw footage into a cleaner, more emotional final narrative.

03 / Camera

Camera for cinematic frames.

Every story begins with a frame. I use a camera that helps capture the texture of a place, the mood of a moment and the energy of the journey.

04 / Gimbal

DJI OM 4 for smooth motion.

Smooth movement and clean transitions make the travel footage feel more alive and immersive, especially when walking through moments, streets and landscapes.

05 / Audio

Microphones that bring clarity.

Sound matters just as much as visuals. Good audio adds depth, emotion and atmosphere to each story and keeps the viewer fully in it.

06 / Tripod

Tripod for steady framing.

Stability matters when the scene needs calm, precise composition. A tripod keeps the frame intentional and gives the footage a polished, controlled finish.

07 / Lighting

Lights to shape the mood.

Lighting adds depth and atmosphere. It helps every subject feel more alive, cinematic and visually rich, especially when the story depends on mood as much as motion.
